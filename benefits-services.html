Patient & Family Resources

Benefits & Services

How we're supporting GBS and CIDP patients on the ground in Nepal — through hospital partnerships, direct patient outreach, and our growing Neuro AI Research Fellowship.

Hospital Collaborations

Ongoing

The President has already established partnerships with three major hospitals in Kathmandu. Whenever a new GBS patient is identified at these hospitals, the hospital contacts our foundation — our President and team members then visit the patient in person to provide mental and emotional support, encouragement, motivation, and any necessary assistance.

01

TU Teaching Hospital

Tribhuvan University Teaching Hospital (TUTH) is the premier public medical and referral center in Nepal, located in Maharajgunj, Kathmandu.

Overview & History
  • Establishment: Opened in March 1986 following a 1981 cooperation agreement with the Japanese government.
  • Capacity: Expanded from an initial 301 beds to roughly 750–850 beds today.
  • Affiliation: Serves as the primary clinical teaching ground for the Institute of Medicine (IOM) under Tribhuvan University.
Key Functions
  • Medical Education: Trains undergraduate and postgraduate students in medicine, nursing, dentistry, and allied health sciences.
  • Healthcare Services: Operates 22 specialized departments — ranging from general emergency care (24/7) to advanced procedures like open-heart surgery, neurology, and kidney transplants.
  • Research: Functions as a leading national hub for biomedical and health sciences research.
02

KMC (Kathmandu Medical College)

Kathmandu Medical College (KMC) is a medical education and healthcare institution in Nepal, affiliated with Kathmandu University. Established in 1997, KMC operates teaching hospitals in Sinamangal and Duwakot, Kathmandu, providing medical education, specialized healthcare, and clinical training.

Overview & History
  • Establishment: Founded in 1997.
  • Affiliation: Affiliated with Kathmandu University.
  • Locations: Teaching hospitals in Sinamangal and Duwakot, Kathmandu.
  • Focus: Combines medical education, patient care, clinical training, and research.
Key Functions
  • Healthcare Services: Provides care across a wide range of specialties, including Neurology, Neurosurgery, Internal Medicine, Cardiology, Nephrology, Psychiatry, and Physiotherapy.
  • Neurological Care: Has dedicated Neurology and Neurosurgery services for patients with neurological and neurosurgical conditions.
  • Emergency & Critical Care: Provides emergency and critical-care services for patients requiring immediate or specialized treatment.
  • Medical Education: Provides undergraduate and postgraduate medical education and clinical training.
  • Diagnostics: Offers diagnostic services and medical technologies to support patient evaluation and treatment.
  • Research & Training: Supports clinical research, medical education, and specialist training, including postgraduate training in Neurosurgery.
03

Neuro Bansabari Hospital (Neuro Hospital)

Neuro Bansbari Hospital, also known as the Upendra Devkota Memorial National Institute of Neurological and Allied Sciences, is a specialized neurological hospital located in Bansbari, Kathmandu. Established in 2006 by the late Professor Dr. Upendra Devkota, the hospital focuses on the diagnosis, treatment, and rehabilitation of neurological and neurosurgical conditions.

Overview & History
  • Establishment: Founded in 2006.
  • Location: Bansbari, Kathmandu, Nepal.
  • Specialization: Focuses primarily on neurology, neurosurgery, and related neurological conditions.
  • Purpose: Established to provide specialized neurological care and advanced treatment within Nepal.
Key Functions
  • Neurology: Provides diagnosis and treatment for conditions affecting the brain, spinal cord, and nervous system.
  • Neurosurgery: Provides surgical treatment for brain and spinal disorders.
  • Emergency & Critical Care: Offers emergency neurological services and intensive care for serious conditions.
  • Diagnostics: Provides neurological testing and imaging to assist with diagnosis and treatment.
  • Rehabilitation: Offers physiotherapy and rehabilitation services to support recovery after neurological conditions and surgery.
  • Research & Training: Supports medical education, specialist training, and neurological research.

Coordination with Bir Hospital, a major government hospital, is currently underway.

Nationwide Outreach

We have also begun reaching out to hospitals across Nepal by sending letters to establish coordination for GBS and CIDP awareness programs and to facilitate the collection of data on GBS patients.

Patient Engagement & Outreach

Enrollment Underway

We personally reach out to patients, listen to their emotional challenges, and reassure them they are not alone — while explaining the foundation and its mission.

WhatsApp & Facebook Community Groups

Community group chats where patients can connect, share their feelings, and offer mutual support. Patient enrollment is currently underway.

Data Collection Phase

We are currently in the data collection phase, gathering and organizing patient information ahead of bringing patients together for structured support.

01
Direct outreach & listening

Our team reaches out individually to each patient, listens to their emotional challenges, and reassures them they are not alone.

02
Community connection

Patients are invited into WhatsApp and Facebook community groups to connect and support one another.

03
Data collection & organization

Patient information is collected and organized to prepare for structured group sessions.

04
Weekly / biweekly expert-led sessions

Once organized, patients join virtual sessions led by medical experts — doctors, consultants, healthcare workers, and physiotherapists — where GBS patients and caregivers can share their feelings, challenges, and stories.

Neuro AI Research Fellowship

Launching

Thank you, GBS International, for supporting us through the Global Program Fund. Your generous support is helping our operations and expanding our work significantly.

Neuro AI Research Fellowship

In partnership with UNESCO, the foundation is launching the Neuro AI Research Fellowship to train doctors, nurses, and healthcare workers in the practical use of AI in healthcare and research.

Through this fellowship, we are working to increase awareness of GBS and CIDP among healthcare professionals across Nepal.
